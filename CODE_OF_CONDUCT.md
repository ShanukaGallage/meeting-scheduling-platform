# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making this project a welcoming, respectful, and productive environment for everyone on the team — regardless of experience level, background, or role.

---

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in code reviews and discussions
- Give and receive feedback professionally — critique the code, not the person
- Help teammates who are stuck rather than leaving them blocked
- Communicate proactively — if you're going to miss a deadline or hit a blocker, say so early
- Keep discussions in the agreed channels (Slack / Jira comments / PR reviews)

---

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissive comments
- Pushing to `main` without a PR review
- Ignoring review comments without explanation
- Sharing access credentials or `.env` files in any shared channel

---

## Reporting Issues

If something feels wrong or unfair, raise it directly with the team lead. All reports are handled respectfully and confidentially.

---

## Enforcement

Repeated violations of this code of conduct may result in removal from the project repository.
